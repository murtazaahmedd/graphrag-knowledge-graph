# Northwind Labs platform overview

Northwind Labs runs a small set of internal services that power billing, analytics and customer support.
This page is the starting point for new engineers.

The Engineering Department contains every product team. The Data Department contains the analytics teams.
The Payments Team is part of the Engineering Department. The Platform Team is part of the Engineering Department.
The Analytics Team is part of the Data Department. The Support Tools Team is part of the Engineering Department.
